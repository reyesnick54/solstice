# Economic rehearsal findings

Economic stress findings (`EconomicStressFinding`) are integrated with
Chunk 70 launch findings.

Any unresolved critical accounting or safety issue becomes
`MAINNET_ENGINEERING_BLOCKER`.

Accepted limitations after a successful rehearsal include:

- External legal, regulatory, licensing, and human production
  authorization remain incomplete.
- Simulation HSM is not a commercial production HSM.
- Production oracle agreements are not fabricated.
- Tickers remain `NOT_ASSIGNED`.
- The production-candidate allocation remains zero/unapproved.
- An extended multi-epoch run is a manual workflow and is not claimed
  unless executed.

Engineering result states
(`ECONOMIC_REHEARSAL_INCOMPLETE`,
`ECONOMIC_REHEARSAL_COMPLETED_WITH_FINDINGS`,
`ECONOMIC_ENGINEERING_REHEARSAL_QUALIFIED`) do not authorize production.
