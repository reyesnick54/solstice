# Chunk 44 — Global Productive Capacity Graph and MoonRey issuance

Implemented on latest `main` after Chunk 40. Chunks 41–43 were not
yet merged on this tip. This chunk does **not** reimplement Money,
the Compliance Kernel, Execution Authority, the Evidence Vault, or
the canonical Ledger. It does **not** create `packages/moonrey` or
`packages/moonrey-coin`.

Canonical owner remains `packages/sunrey-chain`.

- TypeScript engine: `packages/sunrey-chain/src/productive/`
- Local-node crate: `packages/sunrey-chain/rust/crates/productive`
- CLI: `sunrey-node productive …` and `sunrey-node moonrey …`

Public tickers remain `NOT_ASSIGNED`. `ENVIRONMENT` remains
`simulation`. Arbitrary `NATIVE_ASSET ISSUE` of MoonRey stays
inactive. Only a finalized `MoonReyIssuanceAuthorization` derived
from a `VerifiedProductiveContribution` may issue development units.

## Economic model

SunRey remains the human / community / information side of the
future economy. MoonRey represents productive economic activity
increasingly generated by AI, robots, machines, energy systems,
manufacturing, physical resources, infrastructure, real-estate use
capacity, food production, compute, services, and automated output.

MoonRey Coin is not an arbitrary minted token. Issuance is connected
to verified productive activity and capacity.

## Authority model

The Global Productive Capacity Graph is a **derived projection**.
Authoritative blockchain facts are:

- registered productive economic objects
- rights
- oracle facts
- productive claims
- verified contributions
- MoonRey issuance transactions and receipts

The graph may be deleted and rebuilt from finalized history. Graph
rebuild does not change blockchain state.

## Categories

First-class categories: `ENERGY`, `FOOD_AGRICULTURE`, `WATER`,
`MINERALS_RAW_MATERIALS`, `REAL_ESTATE_USE`, `COMPUTE`,
`AI_COMPUTE`, `MANUFACTURING`, `LOGISTICS_TRANSPORTATION`,
`STORAGE`, `BANDWIDTH_COMMUNICATIONS`, `INFRASTRUCTURE`, `GOODS`,
`SERVICES`, `AUTOMATED_MACHINE_OUTPUT`.

Governed extension uses `CategoryExtension` (parent category, unit
schema, activation height). Extension is not an ungoverned enum edit.

## Claim types

`CAPACITY`, `OUTPUT`, `DELIVERY`, `USAGE`, and `RESERVE` stay
distinct. A factory capable of 1,000 units is capacity. 700 units
produced is output. 600 units delivered is delivery. Policy does
not count those as 2,300 units of production.

Development policy (`ENGINEERING_SIMULATION_PARAMETERS`) sets
capacity and delivery claim weights to `0` and does not treat
delivery as independent of output.

## Pipeline

`ProductiveClaim` → verification → `VerifiedProductiveContribution`
→ anti-double-counting (`ContributionFingerprint`) → issuance
policy evaluation → `MoonReyIssuanceAuthorization` → native asset
issuance transaction → finalized MoonRey supply.

Only a finalized authorization issues. The same fingerprint cannot
be issued twice.

## Formula

This remains the `LEGACY_ENGINEERING_SIMULATION_V1` path. Chunk 125
adds a separate `GOVERNED_VALUE_SIMULATION_V2` conversion from GPUV
through a settlement policy into Chunk 71. The formula is not replaced.

Version `moonrey.issuance.formula.v1`:

```
eligible quantity
× category policy weight
× claim policy factor
× quality factor
= MoonRey eligible quantity
```

Integer / fixed-point only (`WEIGHT_SCALE = 1_000_000`). Explicit
rounding: `FLOOR`, `CEIL`, `ROUND_HALF_EVEN`. No floating point.

Category weights belong to versioned governed policy. Development
fixtures are labeled `ENGINEERING_SIMULATION_PARAMETERS`. They are
not market prices or economic promises.

## Units

Chunk 44 consumes a `UnitRegistry` contract (Chunk 43 binding).
Normalization is category-specific. Unrelated economic dimensions
are never converted into one fake universal physical unit.

## Supply

MoonRey development supply follows the Chunk 41 invariant:

`issued − burned = current protocol holdings / locked supply`

Every issuance receipt reconciles with `NativeAssetSupplyState`.
This is not a second fiat ledger and not a customer-journal path.

## Corrections

Finalized history is not silently rewritten. A correction record
may affect future eligibility, future policy, future issuance, or
dispute review. Historical valid issuance is not erased without an
explicit governed corrective transaction.

## Concentration

Policy mechanisms exist for per-object limits, per-controller
limits, per-category epoch limits, per-geography monitoring, and
oracle-source concentration metrics. There is no discrimination
on protected human traits.

## What this chunk does not implement

- A public MoonRey ticker or production MoonRey Coin product
- Arbitrary minting outside verified contributions
- A mutable graph database as blockchain source of truth
- Customer fiat journals or a second Money type
- Counsel-confirmed economic values (`RESEARCH_REQUIRED`)
- Mainnet, live oracles, or live energy/compute providers

`ENVIRONMENT` remains `simulation`. Every `LIVE_*` flag remains false.
