# Dual-economy stability

Stability signals are **engineering classifications**:

- `HEALTHY_SIMULATION`
- `LIQUIDITY_STRESS`
- `ISSUANCE_CONCENTRATION`
- `PRODUCTIVE_CONCENTRATION`
- `ORACLE_DEPENDENCY`
- `FEE_PRESSURE`
- `DEMAND_IMBALANCE`
- `SUPPLY_GROWTH_WARNING`

They are not price forecasts and never use labels such as guaranteed
value, expected guaranteed return, or certain appreciation.

Liquidity metrics: spread, depth, turnover, price impact, volume,
SunRey liquidity, MoonRey liquidity.

Velocity is `period_flow * INDEX_SCALE / circulating` for each asset
separately.

`DUAL_ECONOMY_MODELING` readiness tracks simulator implementation,
baseline run, stress scenarios, and policy comparison as engineering
evidence. Economic review and human approval remain `NOT_PROVIDED`.
This is not production authorization.
