# Monetary governance

Changes to issuance modes, supply constraints, burn rules, fee
eligibility, genesis policy, and distribution-category rules must
use existing SunRey protocol governance.

## AI

AI may model, analyze, and recommend.

AI cannot vote, approve, activate, or authorize production issuance.

## Version activation

Protocol-relevant monetary policy versions become active at an
explicit governed height or epoch. Historical transactions remain
evaluated under the policy active at their finalized height.

## Policy history

Enough history is persisted to verify historic issuance, burns,
locks, and supply reports after future upgrades.

## Burn policy

Authorized classes: `VOLUNTARY_USER_BURN`, `FEE_BURN`,
`PROTOCOL_ECONOMIC_PENALTY`. Validator misconduct must never burn
unrelated customer assets.
