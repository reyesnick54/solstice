# Human economic layer (SunRey)

The human layer is modeled with **aggregate, synthetic** activity:

- human participation
- community activity
- human information rights
- consumer demand
- creative contribution
- entrepreneurial contribution
- governed human-economic distributions
- human use of AI / productive services

No real user personal information is required or stored.

`HumanEconomicParticipationIndex` is a scenario-level aggregate. It does
not score individual human value.

Humans may consume compute, AI service, energy, manufactured goods,
logistics, storage, and other productive services. Demand is synthetic.
