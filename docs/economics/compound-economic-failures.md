# Compound economic failures

Compound scenarios combine independent shocks. They do not invent a
second accounting system to keep numbers looking continuous.

## ECON-COMP-001

Energy scarcity + compute shortage + MoonRey liquidity shock.

Expected: productive issuance fail-closes or shrinks; synthetic market
impact is measured; supplies still reconcile.

## ECON-COMP-002

Oracle outage + Exchange volatility + validator unavailability.

Expected: MoonRey fail-closes; DVP remains atomic; a jailed or penalized
validator does not debit customer holdings.

## ECON-COMP-003

Fee congestion + custody backlog + machine commerce surge.

Expected: skipped transactions are availability, not hidden mint;
custody does not blindly resubmit; machine mandates still hold.

Recovery after each compound run rebuilds Explorer supply from the
canonical books. See `docs/economics/economic-recovery.md`.
