# SunRey Coin policy

SunRey Coin is the native asset of the **human economic layer**.

Its architecture may later represent authorized economic contribution
from human participation, community activity, information rights,
consent-scoped economic contribution, and other governed
human-economic sources.

## Hard boundaries

- No human-worth score.
- No social-credit system.
- Arbitrary AI scoring cannot mint SunRey Coin.
- Raw personal information is not placed on chain.
- PDV and Clean Room source data are not exposed.
- Data entering PDV, Consent, Clean Room, or the Human Information
  Market does not automatically create SunRey Coin.

## Issuance classes

- `GENESIS_ONLY`
- `GOVERNED_ISSUANCE`
- `AUTHORIZED_HUMAN_ECONOMIC_CONTRIBUTION`

Production activation remains `UNCONFIGURED`. Development and test
simulations may exercise the interfaces.

Human-economic evidence stores only a privacy-safe evidence ID,
policy version, authorization, content hash, quantity basis, and
purpose class.
